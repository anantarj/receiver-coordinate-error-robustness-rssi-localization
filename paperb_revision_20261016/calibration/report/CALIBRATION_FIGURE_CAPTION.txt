Validation-selected calibration versus freezing on fit-only inputs. Each marker denotes one of 16 fixed field/fold conditions per magnitude and estimator. All 32 positive-displacement conditions are retained for each estimator, including the four one-kilometre conditions that select no update and overlap at the origin; no jitter is added. Benefits equal frozen minus selected loss on both axes: positive horizontal/vertical values indicate lower target RSS MAE/lower full-cohort P50 positioning error under the selected policy. RSS assessment uses held-out selected receptions on the same 500-query cohorts. RSS-NLS bars bound P50 benefit over found candidate pools within 0.01 dB^2; they are not confidence intervals or exhaustive near-optimal regions. Both axes are relative changes, not absolute adequacy or accuracy certification.
