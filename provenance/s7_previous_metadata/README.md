## Scientific scope

The package retains the original four-pipeline corruption-load, participation and spatial experiments, and adds a WCL-only paired extension: R29/O32-minus-BS71, top one/two, 5 km, 32 specified fields each. There are 128 matched pairs, not 128 independent deployments. Four official-map top-one fields are harmful under refitting and are retained. The original two-field result and the new 32-field summary are different experiments/aggregation sets, not interchangeable numbers.

The primary recovered map is a frozen historical assignment with incomplete selection provenance, not independently surveyed truth. Catalogue controls do not certify physical accuracy. G4 and G6 remain failed. The common-27 official-named map retains the recovered BS71 coordinate in both arms. Source history and the distinction between calibration-state effects and isolated support-exhaustion causality are preserved.
